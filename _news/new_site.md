With a heavy heart I have retired the old site, including the character wiki in preparation for the first volumes release early next year.

The new website will be built from ground up with more copyright friendly assets. In time it will be filled with properly licensed art.

The character wiki being removed was to prevent spoilers primarily, however it will be replaced with a cast system which will show up to date information about characters per volume!
